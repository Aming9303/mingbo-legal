Clear Speaker Support

Clear Speaker is an offline sound utility for muffled speakers after light moisture or dust. It plays locally generated low-frequency tones, sweeps, and pulses.

How to use

  • Start with moderate volume.
  • Keep the speaker facing down while tones play.
  • Do not hold the device near your ear during playback.
  • If your device has serious water exposure or hardware damage, contact Apple or a qualified repair provider.

Privacy

The app does not collect data, does not require an account, and works offline.
